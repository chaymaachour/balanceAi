Personalized planning

Relaxation

Guided breathing and a 5-4-3-2-1 grounding exercise to reset your body and mind in minutes.

BalanceAI offers general well-being support, not medical diagnosis or therapy. If you feel in danger or overwhelmed for a long time, please reach out to a qualified professional, your university counselling service or emergency services (Tunisia: SAMU 190).
