# BalanceAI — 6-slide presentation content

## Slide 1 — The problem
**"I don't even know where to start."**
- Exams, PFE/thesis, job search, family expectations: stress piles up for young women in Tunisian higher education and recent graduates — and for many others.
- Stressed people struggle to (1) name what overwhelms them, (2) decide what to do first, (3) keep healthy habits.
- Professional support can be hard to access or feel intimidating; productivity apps ignore emotions; generic chatbots give generic answers.

*Visual:* a student surrounded by sticky notes → "Overwhelmed".

## Slide 2 — Our solution: BalanceAI
**"A little more balance, one day at a time."**
A calm, private, multilingual web space that turns "I feel overwhelmed" into "here is my next small step":
1. 💜 **Check-in** — mood + context + your own words (EN / FR / Derja / Arabizi).
2. 🧠 **AI understanding** — possible emotions, stress signals, practical challenges, personalised advice.
3. 📅 **Gentle AI planner** — a realistic plan with breaks; never crams an overwhelmed user.
4. 🌬️ **Relax** — guided breathing + 5-4-3-2-1 grounding.
5. 🌱 **Progress** — mood week, habits, simple patterns from your own data.
6. 🗓️ **Studies (Lycée / Université)** — editable ready-made timetable, clubs, cancelled classes, exams, a revision plan placed only in real free time, missed-session rescheduling.
7. 🔔 **Gentle reminders** — end-of-day check-in after the way home and a rest delay, revision reminders, exam encouragement in French, English or Tunisian Derja; in-app centre + real browser notifications.

*Visual:* screenshot of the home page.

## Slide 3 — AI innovation
**Not a chatbot wrapper — a pipeline.**
User input → AI analysis → structured emotional insights → personalised recommendations → AI-generated daily plan → user feedback.
- Understands **Tunisian Derja & Arabizi** as well as French and English.
- **Explainable:** every emotion comes with a confidence level and quotes the user's own words as evidence.
- **Extraction:** tasks mentioned in the text flow automatically into the planner; stress level adapts session length and breaks.
- **Safe by design:** non-diagnostic prompts, backend validation of every AI field, multilingual crisis screen that works even if the AI is down, transparent demo mode.
- Provider-agnostic (Gemini / Groq / OpenAI / local models) — key never leaves the server.
- **AI + deterministic engine:** the AI coach turns an exam into small steps adapted to the days left; a tested scheduler guarantees sessions never overlap classes or sleep.

## Slide 4 — User journey (Amira, 23, final-year student in Tunis)
1. Opens BalanceAI at 14:00, feels 😵‍💫 *overwhelmed*, selects *Studies*.
2. Writes in French + Derja: PFE report in 10 days, two exams, sleeps 5 hours, parents asking about internships.
3. AI reflects: *pressure, worry about the future, fatigue* — quoting "je dors à peine 5 heures" — and suggests 3 concrete steps + one 15-minute next step.
4. One click: "Turn this into a plan" → AI builds a 4-hour plan with 25-min sessions, breaks, a walk, and postpones what doesn't fit — kindly.
5. Ticks off her first block 🎉, takes a 1-minute breathing break, and sees her mood week improve.

## Slide 5 — Expected impact
- **For users:** less paralysis, clearer priorities, more breaks and sleep, a non-judgmental first step toward support.
- **Accessible:** free, no account, works on any phone browser, French/English UI, Derja/Arabizi input.
- **Private:** data stays on the device; one-click deletion.
- **Bridge, not replacement:** encourages users to reach university counselling or professionals when stress is heavy or persistent; emergency guidance (SAMU 190) when needed.
- **Measurable (pilot ideas):** check-ins per week, % of focus blocks completed, self-reported stress before/after breathing, 👍/👎 feedback on AI insights.
